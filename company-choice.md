## My AI Agent Idea

I chose **Nexova Solutions** for my final AI Engineering project because AI is positioned as a competitive advantage in the company's core services, not just as an internal productivity tool. Its briefing describes a broad range of concrete AI opportunities across recruitment, training, sales, and customer support. This gives me room to build a focused system while still applying and evaluating meaningful AI techniques. I will keep the project assistive, with people making final decisions where candidate or client outcomes are involved.

The two departments I find most interesting are:

1. **Talent Selection Operations**: This is Nexova's core business, and its 40 consultants currently review 30 to 80 CVs per search manually. Candidate search, CV scoring, and ranking could help consultants identify relevant profiles, provided results are explainable and reviewed by a person.
2. **Corporate Training**: The catalogue and enrolment process rely on a PDF, Google Forms, and spreadsheets, with little personalization or completion tracking. A recommendation system or training-plan assistant could better match programmes to each client's needs.

The milestone-map challenge I am most looking forward to building is a **RAG system for semantic search over the candidate database**. Recruiters could ask for profiles matching criteria such as B2B sales experience and C1 English, then review the evidence behind each result. This challenge is explicitly identified in the Nexova briefing and aligns with the RAG milestone; I would evaluate retrieval quality on a test set and keep candidate selection under human control.
