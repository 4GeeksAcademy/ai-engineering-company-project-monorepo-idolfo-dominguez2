# Project Brief

## Company

Nexova Solutions is a human-resources consulting and talent-acquisition firm founded in 2011, headquartered in Valencia, Spain, with an expansion office in Miami, Florida. It has about 120 employees and serves mostly mid-sized technology, retail, and financial-services clients through executive and mid-management recruitment, outsourced customer support, and corporate training.

## Business Problem

Nexova's reputation and expertise have outgrown its disconnected, largely manual operations. Talent Selection is its core business: 40 consultants manage recruitment from client briefings through CV screening, interviews, and placement, and each consultant currently reads 30 to 80 CVs per search. Candidate matching relies on consultant judgment, communication is sent through individual emails, and clients lack real-time candidate-status visibility. Elsewhere, training enrolment relies on a PDF catalogue, Google Forms, and spreadsheets; support averages 48-hour resolution against a 24-hour SLA; sales follow-up is inconsistent in HubSpot; and leadership receives weekly PDFs prepared manually and already several days old. These processes consume staff time, limit client visibility, and make it harder to scale consistently.

## Project Objectives

The company-wide objective is to build data-driven systems and AI-enabled tools that help Nexova deliver its existing services faster and at greater scale, with less manual effort.

The selected project direction is an AI-assisted talent-selection system, beginning with semantic search over Nexova's candidate profiles. A recruiter should be able to search from a client recruitment brief (for example, B2B sales experience and C1 English), inspect profile evidence supporting each result, and prioritize which CVs to review among the 30 to 80 typically screened per search. This is decision support only: consultants retain responsibility for interviews, candidate communication, and final placement decisions.

## Intended Users and Value

- Selection consultants need to find relevant candidates more quickly across the database.
- Clients benefit from a more efficient recruitment process and clearer, more consistent candidate matching.
- Nexova benefits from reduced manual screening effort and a scalable, measurable workflow.

## Success Considerations

Measure retrieval quality against search criteria and relevance labels reviewed by selection consultants. Results should be relevant, traceable to candidate-profile evidence, and useful in the existing recruitment workflow. Candidate data is sensitive: use only approved, minimized data, preserve client boundaries, and do not infer hiring decisions from ranking scores.
